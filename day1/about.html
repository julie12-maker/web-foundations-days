About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple tool for keeping track of important ideas and reminders. It helps users organize information and quickly find the notes they need.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes.
  2. Write down your important idea or reminder.
  3. Review your notes whenever you need them.

Features

  • Simple note-taking
  • Easy organization
  • Quick access to notes

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save a note
